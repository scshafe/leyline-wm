# Model and observed layout

Leyline keeps an intended arrangement and a separately observed world. A canonical
model, accepted setter, matching readback, submitted border and physically visible
frame establish different facts.

## Determinism and ownership

The recursive allocation model derives owner-local cells, nested membership,
constraints and projected frames. Every eligible member appears exactly once,
including overload members sharing one footprint. No-hole geometry and membership
conservation are independent checks. Minima compose upward; frames project down
through the same model. Spacing derives once from the configured border width;
requested origins and opposite edges round together.

The same model, trustworthy world and causally ordered admitted intent produce the
same pure decision/targets. Pixel-identical layouts can retain different nested
ownership and future behavior. A presented overload top does not erase its members.
The derived owner hierarchy is a view, not a second writable arrangement store.

A complete identity-bound observed world is required for authoring. The partition
owner preserves inactive/OFF state and checks current membership before pruning or
adopting. A damaged journal or partial observation cannot supply a plausible empty
model.

## Effects and presentation

One candidate carries its desired arrangement, rounded affected frames, ordered
participants and base provenance. Preview keeps actual windows stationary; commit
consumes that candidate or publishes a replacement requiring fresh confirmation.
The controller binds current authority and journals the transition. The effector
issues bounded AX goals and requires matching lifetime/context readback. Partial
physical effects retain their actual outcome and committed checkpoint.

| Boundary | Establishes |
|---|---|
| Complete current observation | Genuine eligible members, context and observed frames |
| Pure plan/candidate | Intended arrangement and exact requested targets |
| Journaled authority commit | Accepted model transition and immutable revision |
| Issued setter | Scoped external effect attempt |
| Matching landed readback | Verified compound goal under current identity/context |
| AppKit submission | Submitted immutable presentation plan |
| Independent physical evidence | A measured visible/scanout endpoint with separate provenance |

Later observations retain original receipt, lifetime, context and generation.
Changed authority fences stale completions and pending work. Known-floor repair
and newly learned clamps require actual writes and the prescribed later reads;
unchanged cached values cannot establish minima.

Model/planner/command/effect/Space/presentation tests verify geometry, conservation,
constraints, preview equality, invalidation and publication ordering. Independent
frozen oracles are test inputs. Genuine physical input, visibility, Space identity,
trust and recovery evidence remain governed by [responsiveness](contracts/event-driven-responsiveness.md).
