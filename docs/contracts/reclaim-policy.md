# Reclaim and close refocus

The product uses one fixed even reclaim policy. Removing a managed window changes
only its recursive owner and conserves each surviving identity exactly once,
including nested and overload members. No selectable reclaim strategy exists.

[ArrangementAllocation](../../leylined/Sources/LeylineModel/ArrangementAllocation.swift)
locates the member and delegates to
[ArrangementPrune](../../leylined/Sources/LeylineModel/ArrangementPrune.swift).
The resident planner authors one layout/focus goal, the controller journals it,
and the effector verifies external results.

## Freed-space redistribution

The even candidate re-spaces the eligible contiguous survivor run in its owning
segment bag, trying a row run before a column run. Raw partition cells remain
hole-free; border spacing is projected once. Known physical minima constrain each
share. Every candidate must satisfy valid geometry, exact survivor re-keying and
membership conservation. A flat removal removes exactly that cell. Removing an
obscured overload member preserves its shared cell and other members. Unaffected
owners and nested identities retain their structure.

Pinned boundaries remain deliberate constraints. If pinned interior dividers
prevent an even spread, the same author considers valid local/grow candidates.
Unavailable authoring refuses rather than publishing holes, invented fit or lost
members. Nested minima compose upward and frames project downward.

## Confirmed focus

Close or minimize preserves valid managed focus. Otherwise prefer an eligible
reclamation absorber, then the most recently confirmed eligible survivor from a
deduplicated depth-10 history. All candidates must match their current lifetime
and enabled visible Space context. No survivor means no focus effect.

[ResidentPlannerLayout](../../leylined/Sources/leylined/ResidentPlannerLayout.swift)
selects the target. The controller records confirmed focus after a positively
verified focus read or compound frame/focus completion; setter acceptance alone
cannot update history. An even spread may have no single absorber; `absorber:null`
then uses history or produces no focus effect. Overload-member removal preserves
an explicit presented top.

Native and independent frozen regression checks cover no holes, conservation,
pinned boundaries, nested/overload survival, valid-focus retention, absorber/recent
selection, stale history and no-survivor behavior. Actual effects and visible timing
remain separate [evidence boundaries](event-driven-responsiveness.md).
