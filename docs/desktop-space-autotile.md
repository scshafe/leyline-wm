# Desktop Space management

Eligibility, visibility and saved ON/OFF preference are separate facts. Leyline
manages only identifiable enabled visible ordinary desktop Spaces. Each identified
Space defaults ON and retains its own choice. Inactive/OFF partitions remain stored
without current background authoring or effects.

## Captured controls

Hyper + A toggles the focused identifiable Space, including an OFF or empty Space
under complete trusted observation. It is global-first in default/move/resize and
toggles once per press. A queued press cannot control a newly focused Space.
Opening the menu freezes its Space/display target; later focus changes cannot
retarget a selection. Unknown identity makes the control unavailable.

Space OFF invalidates its interactions and removes its overlays while preserving
actual frames. Other enabled visible Spaces, the resident and menu remain active.
Space ON requires a fresh complete baseline and accepted reconciliation before
input readiness. Failed convergence cannot restore stale frames or report ready.

Global desired ON/OFF persists, with ON the absent-settings default. Invalid or
unreadable settings block readiness. A temporary trust/readiness failure parks
capabilities without changing desired state. Global OFF stops all management but
retains mouse controls; launchd Disable is a separate whole-process operation.

## Identity and conservation

The native observer independently baselines desktop Space UUIDs/display context
and validates each window's membership. Runtime counters, display indices and
window-cohort overlap cannot supply durable identity. Incomplete data, ambiguous
membership, unsupported system Spaces and unavailable bridge capabilities refuse
observation rather than guessing. Failed AX reads do not prove ineligibility.

The partition owner requires complete world and current lifetime/UUID/display
membership. A partial world cannot prune a partition as empty. A switch into an
empty Space cannot delete the previously visible Space. Every eligible nested or
obscured overload member remains conserved once in its own partition.

## Visible-context relocation

After an OS-completed mouse drag between concurrently visible display-local Spaces,
fresh trustworthy observation can reclaim the enabled source and adopt into an
enabled destination in one guarded settle. OFF partitions remain untouched.
Keyboard swap/insert likewise requires both participating Spaces to be visible,
enabled and identifiable. A staged span stays within one display owner.

Leyline has no OS Space-transfer actuator. Inactive/background transfer is
unsupported; a model edit cannot establish that macOS moved a window. Actual Space
identity across display/login changes requires its own live evidence rather than
fixture success. See [move](contracts/move-mode-spec.md) and
[responsiveness](contracts/event-driven-responsiveness.md).
